How it works: scroll or search the list on the left, click a material, type new prices (only the lines you want to change) or use Hide / Restore, press Stage these changes. "+ Add a new material" creates one as a copy of an existing material. "+ Add a new metal" adds a metal to the Alternative or Precious tab (you type its prices; precious golds price themselves by the live formula). Metals also get Hide as Base Metal / Hide as Inlay buttons to hide them from just one place. "$ Fixed metal prices" sets a fixed market price per precious metal — ring pricing always uses the HIGHER of the live spot price and the fixed price. Repeat as needed, then Submit for approval on the right. Johan reviews and publishes — nothing goes live until he approves.
